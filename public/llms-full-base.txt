## 5. FAQs (from site pages, condensed)

General (https://www.sharprays.com/, /contact, /work)
- Cost? Depends on needs; goals and scope come first.
- Startups? Yes, and established businesses too. A single service is fine.
- Guarantees? No promised numbers; strategy, execution and transparent reporting instead.
- Existing website or internal team? Yes, both possible.
- Are all projects client work? No; projects are labelled Client, Internal or Concept.

Pricing (https://www.sharprays.com/pricing)
- Monthly cost in India? Single-service plans from ₹9,999/month; most growing businesses combine 2–3 services.
- Ad spend included? No; the fee covers strategy, setup, management and reporting. Prices exclude taxes.
- Rankings or leads guaranteed? No.
- Best starting package? Usually a website plus one channel (SEO or ads); a free audit is offered.

SEO (https://www.sharprays.com/services/search-engine-optimization)
- Timeline? No universal timeline; depends on site condition, competition and authority.
- First-page guarantee? No.
- AI Overviews? Good SEO helps clarity and discovery, but appearing in AI answers cannot be guaranteed.
- SEO vs Google Ads? Ads give immediate paid visibility; SEO builds long-term organic discovery. Many businesses use both.

Website (https://www.sharprays.com/services/website-development)
- Design and development? Both, as one connected process, scoped before work starts.
- Custom websites? Yes, where the project needs it.

AI automation (https://www.sharprays.com/services/ai-automation)
- What can be automated? Lead capture, CRM updates, support routing, follow-ups, reporting, documents, notifications.
- Replace existing software? Usually not; existing systems are connected.
- Replace employees? Aim is to reduce repetitive work; people stay for judgement and exceptions.
- Cost and timeline? Depend on workflows, integrations and complexity; defined after discovery.

Content (https://www.sharprays.com/services/content-marketing)
- SEO content, website copy, blogs, thought leadership? Yes, within agreed scope.
- AI content? AI may assist; human review and real expertise are required.
- Publishing frequency? No universal cadence; quality over volume.
